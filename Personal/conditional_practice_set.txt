==========================
PYTHON CONDITIONAL STATEMENTS PRACTICE (10 QUESTIONS)
==========================

1. Positive, Negative, or Zero
Question:
Take an integer as input and print whether it is Positive, Negative, or Zero.

Expected Output Example:
Enter a number: -15
Negative Number


--------------------------------------------------

2. Even or Odd
Question:
Take an integer as input and print whether it is Even or Odd.

Expected Output Example:
Enter a number: 24
Even Number


--------------------------------------------------

3. Largest of Three Numbers
Question:
Take three integers as input and print the largest number.

Expected Output Example:
Enter first number: 10
Enter second number: 45
Enter third number: 30

Largest Number = 45


--------------------------------------------------

4. Leap Year Checker
Question:
Take a year as input and determine whether it is a Leap Year.

Expected Output Example:
Enter year: 2024

2024 is a Leap Year


--------------------------------------------------

5. Grade Calculator
Question:
Take marks of five subjects. Calculate the percentage and display the grade.

Expected Output Example:
Percentage = 84.4%

Grade = B


--------------------------------------------------

6. Electricity Bill
Question:
Take the number of electricity units consumed and calculate the bill according to different slabs.

Expected Output Example:
Units Consumed: 180

Total Bill = 1160


--------------------------------------------------

7. ATM Withdrawal
Question:
Take account balance and withdrawal amount. Allow withdrawal only if:
- Balance is sufficient.
- Withdrawal amount is a multiple of 100.
- Minimum balance after withdrawal is 500.

Expected Output Example:
Withdrawal Successful

Remaining Balance = 4200

OR

Insufficient Balance


--------------------------------------------------

8. BMI Calculator
Question:
Take height (meters) and weight (kg). Calculate BMI and print its category.

Expected Output Example:
BMI = 23.4

Category = Normal Weight


--------------------------------------------------

9. Login System
Question:
Store a username and password in the program. Ask the user to enter credentials and validate them.

Expected Output Example:
Login Successful

OR

Invalid Username

OR

Invalid Password


--------------------------------------------------

10. Shopping Discount
Question:
Take the purchase amount and apply the appropriate discount.

Expected Output Example:
Purchase Amount = 6500

Discount = 20%

Final Amount = 5200
